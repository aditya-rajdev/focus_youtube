About & Privacy

Built with privacy and zero data collection at heart.

🔒 100% Privacy Focused

YouTube Focus Mode operates entirely within your browser. It collects zero personal data, tracks no browsing history, sends no network telemetry, and uses no external APIs.

⚡ Performance First

Employs instant CSS-based hiding combined with a lightweight, debounced MutationObserver. No video playback interference, no audio delay, and minimal CPU/memory impact.
